Four analytical lenses

One process. More than one perspective.

Each branch captures a different dimension of procurement behaviour. Their scores are combined into a relative review-priority ranking.

LENS 01 / STRUCTURE

Tabular

Examines duration, award value, procurement method, category and other structured process attributes.

LENS 02 / HISTORY

Temporal

Compares current duration and award-value patterns against strictly prior observations.

LENS 03 / RELATIONSHIPS

Buyer–supplier graph

Uses prior awards, supplier diversity and historical buyer–awarded-supplier relationship features.

LENS 04 / LANGUAGE

Text

Analyses procurement descriptions for unusual wording and patterns in the available tender text.

From source to service

A reproducible pipeline, not a notebook-only experiment.

The trained model is served through a typed FastAPI interface. The browser demo below submits a prepared feature record to the same scoring contract.

01 / INGEST

Responsible use

A signal for reviewers. Not a verdict about suppliers.

Anomaly scores are contextual ranking signals. They should guide where a qualified person looks next, never determine an adverse outcome on their own.

✓
Human review remains centralExamine original procurement documents and independently corroborate any concern.
✓
Only source-supported relationshipsThe graph uses buyer–awarded-supplier history. The source does not provide complete bidder participation data.
✓
Transparent limitationsMissing information is retained and reported; absent bidder data is not invented.
Important interpretation

Unusual does not mean unlawful.

The model produces a relative review-priority percentile. It does not estimate the probability of fraud, prove corruption or collusion, or establish misconduct. Review bands are operational triage categories, not legal classifications.
